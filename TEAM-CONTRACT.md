## How we decide
Small things: whoever is doing the work decides. Big things (scope, stack, pivots, anything that changes `docs/spec.md`): consensus. If we cannot agree within 48 hours, the spec keeper decides, and the decision is written as a record in `docs/decisions/`.

## Pull requests
One person opens the pull request; the other reviews and merges. Nobody pushes to `main` directly. HW pull requests are checked only for staying inside the author's own `hw1/` or `hw2/` folder.

## When we work
Weekly sync: Monday 20:00, Google Meet, 30 minutes. Core hours when we can both be reached on Teams: 18:00 to 22:00 on weekdays.

## Contribution
Work is visible in commits, pull request reviews and each member's journal in `docs/journal/`. If one of us carries or one of us coasts, we say so within the week, not in Week 15.

## When it breaks
Step 1: name it inside the team within 48 hours. Step 2: if it is still unresolved after one week, we bring it to the instructor together. No-show rule: after two missed syncs in a row without notice, the other member emails the instructor.
